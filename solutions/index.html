The three systems

From first page to final move.

Three systems, each taking one part of the work, from the record itself to the paths a matter can take. Every one answers to counsel. None stands in for it.

The record, read and indexed to the page.

PRISM

Precision Retrieval and Intelligent Semantic Mapping

A case file arrives the way Indian matters do: paper and scans, some of it digital, much of it in more than one language, none of it in order. PRISM takes all of it and holds it as one record that answers.

Ask a question and the answer comes back with the page it came from. Counsel checks each claim against the source in seconds, not days.

PRISM holds the whole file as one structure. A line in an annexure connects to the deposition that turns on it, and the connection is there before anyone thinks to look for it.

  • Every answer names the page it came from, so counsel checks it against the source.

  • A record that shifts between languages reads as one. Counsel asks in one language and the answer draws on all of them.

  • A line in an annexure ties to the deposition that turns on it. The link is there before anyone thinks to look for it.

  • A single query draws together the pleadings, exhibits, and correspondence that bear on it.

  • Dates and events across the file assemble into one timeline counsel can follow.

  • Each exhibit and annexure is tied to the document that relies on it.

The authorities, read without rest.

AIR

Autonomous Infinite Research

Precedent in India runs vast and hard to trace. A single point can turn on an authority buried three citations deep, across forums that keep no common system.

AIR reads all of it and researches without rest, so counsel's judgment carries further. It works through the body of authority at a scale no person can, and it does not stop until the question is answered.

The work is guided by counsel and answers to counsel. AIR strengthens the hand that holds it. It never takes its place.

  • Set it a question and it returns with the authorities that govern it, however deep they sit.

  • It follows a judgment through the cases that cite it, forward and back.

  • It shows whether a precedent was followed, distinguished, or overruled.

  • It tracks the statutes and rules that bear on a matter as they change.

  • Research runs across courts and tribunals in a single pass.

  • The work returns as a written memorandum, cited and ready for counsel to weigh.

The board, seen several moves ahead.

SCALE

Simulation and Case Analysis for Legal Evaluation

Indian practice offers no single path. The right court, the place of jurisdiction, the actions open to a matter: each turns on laws that change from one state to the next, and no one can hold all of it at once.

SCALE runs the moves a matter can take and the reactions they draw, and it follows the branches past the point a mind can track. Counsel sees further and decides with greater certainty.

It gives a sharper view of the board, with the risk shown at every turn. The decision stays with counsel.

  • SCALE runs a strategy forward through the moves a matter can take.

  • It models how an opponent is likely to respond at each turn.

  • It maps the branches a case can follow and weighs where each one leads.

  • It shows where a matter is exposed and how much turns on each point.

  • It rehearses the hearing before it happens: the questions likely to come and the arguments that answer them.

  • It weighs the strength of a settlement position against the paths to judgment.
