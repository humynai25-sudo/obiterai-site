About us

The first legal intelligence built in private, for a few.

Obiter is a private legal intelligence. It is not sold, and it is not for everyone. A small number of firms work with it, and the work stays within their walls.

We began with the practice, not the technology. Before any of it was built, we studied where Indian litigation actually breaks. Records that arrive as scattered, multilingual paper. Precedent too vast to trace by hand. A legal system with no natural uniformity from one state to the next. Obiter is built backwards from that.

In a single year we designed it, built it, and put it into real matters. Obiter reads a whole case file and answers from the record. It traces the authorities that govern a question, and it weighs the moves a matter can take before counsel commits to one.

Obiter works for counsel and answers to counsel. The judgment stays human. What Obiter changes is how far that judgment reaches.

Vision

Toward the strongest legal capability in the country.

We are building toward one thing: the most capable legal intelligence in India, kept in the hands of a few.

Obiter today is three systems. It will not stay three. The work ahead deepens each of them and adds new systems for problems the first three were never meant to reach. In time they draw toward a single intelligence a firm runs its hardest matters through.

The reach of that intelligence grows with the people using it. The sharper the lawyers, the further Obiter carries, and the further it carries, the sharper they become.

Where that loop leads is the reason the company exists. What comes next holds the two strands of it.
